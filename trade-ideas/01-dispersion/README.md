Every number in it is generated by the notebook [`dispersion_trade_idea.ipynb`](dispersion_trade_idea.ipynb), and none is typed by hand. The notebook writes this README and the figures when it runs.

**What the notebook does**
- Takes a snapshot of ATM implied vols for SPX and its top-50 components from Yahoo option chains: parity-implied forwards, Black-76, total-variance interpolation to 1M and 3M.
- Computes implied correlation with the Cboe formula and compares it with Cboe COR1M/COR3M and with realized correlation (21d and 63d windows).
- Backtests short 3M correlation struck at COR3M as a daily ladder with approximate mark-to-market, with strike-haircut sensitivity, drawdowns and named stress windows.
- Sizes a theta-neutral straddle dispersion book and reports carry and invalidation levels.
